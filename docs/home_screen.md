# Home screen

The home screen provides Continue my walk (after opening a stage or its map), My Camino plan and Browse stages. The remembered screen is stored locally and reopening it does not start an alert session.

The next planned day is the earliest saved date from today onwards across all plans. If none exists, the first undated day is shown. Past dated days are excluded. Distances, walking times and accommodation status come from the saved plan; they are not recalculated or treated as completed walks. Open this plan allows editing. An empty plan collection offers Create my plan.

My walking day, Ahead of me and Trail elevation remain accessible. The page scrolls and respects device safe areas.
