Map POI icons for the Camino app
================================
These sixteen icons are original artwork drawn for this app as SVG shapes
(fork and knife, cup, glass, mug, burger, trolley, shop front, apple, loaf,
cross, stethoscope, ring and cross, H, drop, two figures, euro coin).
No third-party icon set, font or image was used or traced.

Map data: (c) OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Vector tiles: OpenMapTiles.
Those credits cover the map data, not these icons.
